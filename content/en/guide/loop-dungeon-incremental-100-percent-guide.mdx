## Loop Dungeon Incremental 100 Percent Guide: The Short Version

Most players clear Loop Dungeon Incremental in a single evening and still walk away with locked heroes, unused skills, and a prestige system they never really learned. That is the gap this Loop Dungeon Incremental 100 percent guide is built to close. The premise is simple: lead a party of heroes through repeating dungeon loops, collect loot, buy permanent upgrades, and stack synergies until the dungeon finally runs out of road. Community reports put a first full clear at roughly two to two and a half hours, so 100 percent completion here is not a marathon — it is an efficiency problem. A Loop Dungeon Incremental 100 percent guide is therefore less about grinding and more about sequencing: the order you unlock heroes, the order you spend prestige currency, and the order you clear content.

## Pre-Game Rules That Save You Hours

Before you touch a single upgrade, internalize these five rules. They are the difference between a clean completion run and a restart.

- **Do not prestige on your first wall.** Prestiging early gives you a tiny bonus pool and forces you to re-buy everything you just lost. Player experience consistently describes this as the most punishing part of the game.
- **Pick one carry hero and over-level them.** A single strong damage dealer clears floors faster than an evenly spread party.
- **Read the tooltips after every patch.** The developer shipped a fix for incorrect tooltip information, so old guides and old screenshots may describe effects that no longer work the way you expect.
- **Start a clean save if you played the demo.** Community reports describe a bug where demo saves keep certain skills locked after prestiging in the full version — the Mage is the most commonly cited example.
- **Bank prestige currency before spending it.** Save up, then buy in priority order instead of dribbling the currency away one node at a time.

For a completion run, the practical order is: stabilize with your starting party, unlock the Rogue for burst, then build around Activate once your support slot is online. That sequence alone will carry you through most of the content.

## Prestige Strategy: Spend Like You Mean It

Prestige is the system that decides whether your completion run feels rewarding or tedious. Reviews describe it as "taxing" because the bonus currency is largely spent re-buying upgrades you already had. You can avoid that feeling by following a strict spending priority.

| Priority | Principle | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Buy permanent power before convenience | Compounding stats make every future loop faster |
| 2 | Invest in anything that accelerates the early loop | Faster re-clears mean faster currency for the next prestige |
| 3 | Delay re-buying your old loadout | Re-buying everything immediately leaves you with no forward progress |
| 4 | Prestige only when a run genuinely stalls | Prestiging at every wall shrinks your bonus pool |
| 5 | Keep one hero build untouched | A stable carry prevents post-prestige collapse |

A useful rule of thumb: if you cannot describe what you gained from the last prestige in one sentence, you prestiged too early. The community consensus is that the system rewards patience far more than frequency.

## Fixing the Blockers That Stop a Completion Run

Technical problems can end a 100 percent attempt faster than any boss. Here are the issues reported by players, along with the fixes that actually work.

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Crash on launch with "Failed to load il2cpp" on Windows 11 | Smart App Control blocking the unsigned Unity DLL | Unblock all game files via Properties, update Windows Security definitions, or launch with `-force-d3d11` |
| "This skill is locked, get the full version" after prestige | Demo save carried into the full game | Start a clean save and report the issue to the developer |
| Incorrect tooltip values | Known bug, since patched | Update to the latest build |
| Runs feel slow and clicky | Manual ability casting | Queue abilities during downtime; autocast is a frequently requested feature |

The Windows 11 crash is worth calling out because it looks like a broken install. It is not. The developer confirmed the cause is Windows Smart App Control flagging an unsigned game library, and a code-signed build is the long-term fix. In the meantime, unblocking the files in the game folder is the workaround that resolves it for most players.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does 100 percent completion take?**

Community reports put a first full clear at roughly two to two and a half hours, with completion adding a bit more for prestige cycles and cleanup. This is a compact game — the challenge is sequencing, not raw hours.

**Why are my skills locked after prestiging in the full version?**

Player reports describe a demo-save conflict where certain skills, including the Mage unlock, revert to a "get the full version" state after the first prestige. Starting a fresh save resolves it in most cases.

**Is there anything after the final boss?**

Prestige is the long-term loop. The developer also confirmed that a few secrets were intentionally left undocumented, so exploration after the credits is still worth your time — a good Loop Dungeon Incremental 100 percent guide should always leave room for that.

**Do I need to follow a specific build to hit 100 percent?**

No. The developer explicitly invites players to experiment with hero and skill combinations, and the Priest's Activate skill is designed for creative pairings. Any build that clears bosses comfortably will finish the content.
